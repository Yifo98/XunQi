# 讯栖 XunQi · 栖 — Demo Design System

## Brand truth

- Visual source: `assets/brand/xunqi-qidu-master.png`
- Product screenshots: `assets/screens/xunqi-01.png` through `xunqi-08.png`
- Mood: warm editorial calm, local-first utility, restrained Chinese cultural tone

## Palette

- Paper: `#FFFDF7`
- Paper shadow: `#F3EFE6`
- Ink: `#172033`
- Indigo: `#4F5FE7`
- Indigo dark: `#3E4DC7`
- Indigo haze: `#D8DAFF`
- Gold: `#FFC231`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

- Chinese and product statements: `Noto Sans JP`, 400 / 700
- English metadata and chapter labels: `Space Mono`, 400 / 700
- Display size: 72–104 px
- Caption size: 34–42 px
- Metadata size: 20–24 px

## Motion

- Rhythm: `reveal-HOLD-guided-guided-PROOF-caution-resolve`
- Primary transition: medium blur crossfade, 0.38–0.45 s, `power2.inOut`
- Screenshot motion: slow 1.00 → 1.035 push-in with 12–24 px lateral travel
- Decorative motion: one shared orbit drift plus low-opacity paper grain

## Frame rules

- 1920 × 1080, warm light canvas
- Screenshots remain the focal evidence; no fabricated product screens
- Use visible 2 px structural rules and strong blue-violet accents
- Keep captions in the title-safe area and outside core UI controls
- No neon, full-screen dark gradient, fake browser chrome, or invented product data
